# Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard

A 2-page interactive Power BI dashboard that turns raw retail sales data
(January–June 2023) into clear KPIs, trends and product/regional insights.

## Business Problem
Sales data was stored as raw transactions, which made it hard to quickly answer:
- How much do we sell and earn, and what is our profit margin?
- Which regions and products drive the most sales?
- How do sales and profit change over time, by month and by weekday?
- Which products are the most and least profitable?

## Key Metrics
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | 124,874 |
| Total Profit | 25,820 |
| Profit Margin | 20.68% |
| Total Orders | 120 |
| Avg Order Value | 1.04K |

## Key Insights
- North is the top region (35K); South is the weakest (24K).
- Mouse is the best-selling product (23,243, 18.6% of sales).
- Saturday is the most profitable weekday (6.2K), Thursday the weakest (2.4K).
- Profit margin peaked in April (~24%) and dropped in March (~17.5%).
- Phone has the highest margin (23.11%); Tablet has the lowest (18.54%).

## What I Built
| Page | Content |
|---|---|
| Page 1 – Overview | KPI cards, Sales by Region, Orders by Sale Level, Sales by Products, Sales Trend with metric toggle |
| Page 2 – Detailed Analysis | MTD sales matrix by day and month, Monthly Sales & Profit Margin combo chart, Sales vs Profit by Product, Profit by Weekday, Product Ranking table |

Both pages have Month and Region slicers.
